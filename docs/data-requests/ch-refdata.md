## Before sending

- **Status.** A private non-profit foundation, operated by its subsidiary Refdata Services GmbH
  (Partner-refdatabase guide, version 2/2026; VERIFIED). Not a public body.
- **What is published.** The Partner-refdatabase is free online and as an XML download, described
  as "lizenzfrei", and third parties may take over and enrich the base data (guide, § 5.1); but the
  published fields are only GLN, name, given name, profession, status and a short work address
  (town and canton): **no street, no phone, no specialty** (§ 5.5). Use for advertising is excluded
  (§ 6.2) (VERIFIED). The Refdata disclaimer requires "vorheriger schriftlicher Zustimmung" for
  republication (ADR 0002, CH9): hence this letter.
- **Value.** GLN data help verify claims and link MedReg entries; they cannot build a directory on
  their own (no street address). Low priority next to [ch-medreg.md](ch-medreg.md).

## Letter (English)

**Subject: Request for written consent to republish data of the Partner-refdatabase (GLN)**

Stiftung Refdata
Baarerstrasse 2, 6300 Zug

Dear Sir or Madam,

{{PUBLISHER_NAME}}, {{PUBLISHER_ADDRESS}}, publishes Apercura, a free, non-profit and open-source
directory of health practitioners. Each page shows factual information only, with its source and
date. There are no ratings, no advertising and no paid ranking. Every practitioner can claim their
page to correct or complete it, or object to its publication; an objection is honoured at once and
the entry is never imported again.

**Request.** We ask for your written consent to republish the following data of the
Partner-refdatabase, for active health professionals, on the pages of our directory: GLN, name and
given name, profession, status, and town and canton of practice. We need no other data. The data
will not be used for advertising.

**Licence.** We ask for the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0) or equivalent
terms. Every page would credit "Source: Refdata" with the retrieval date.

**Data protection.** The data are professional data Refdata makes publicly available. Our
processing rests on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); we inform practitioners (Article 14
GDPR and article 19 nFADP, through a notice on every page and in our privacy policy) and honour
their objections (Article 21 GDPR and article 30(2)(b) nFADP).

Thank you in advance for your answer.

Yours faithfully,

{{PUBLICATION_DIRECTOR}}, for {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}
