## Before sending

- **What is already open.** The national catalogue lists "Medical Practitioners (Principal)" and the
  other doctor and dentist registers, published by the Medical Council, under **CC BY 4.0**
  (VERIFIED on data.europa.eu). Its only distribution is the online search
  (`mcmregistration.gov.mt`); there is no file. The request is therefore mainly a **written
  confirmation** that the CC BY 4.0 covers republishing individual entries, plus a machine-readable
  copy.
- **Public sector body: probable, unconfirmed.** The Council is a statutory body under the Health
  Care Professions Act (Cap. 464, art. 10(1)(c): "to keep, publish and update registers"). Five of
  its 13 members are appointed by the Prime Minister, so the board test fails; whether it is
  mainly state-financed or sits within the public administration is UNVERIFIED. The letter rests
  first on the licence the Council itself chose, then on Cap. 546.
- **Address.** Cap. 464 does not list the register fields, and many entries withhold an address.
  The letter asks for a practice address only where the register already publishes one.

## Letter

**Subject: Re-use of the registers of medical practitioners and dentists published under CC BY
4.0: request for written confirmation and a machine-readable copy (Cap. 546)**

To the Registrar, Medical Council Malta,
St Luke's Hospital OPD (Level 1), St Luke's Square, Gwardamangia, Pietà PTA 1010

Dear Registrar,

{{PUBLISHER_NAME}}, {{PUBLISHER_ADDRESS}}, publishes Apercura, a free, non-profit and open-source
directory of health practitioners. Each page shows factual information only, with its source and
date. There are no ratings, no advertising and no paid ranking. Every practitioner can claim their
page to correct or complete it, or object to its publication; an objection is honoured at once and
the entry is never imported again.

The national open data catalogue lists the Council's registers of medical practitioners (principal
and specialist registers) and of dentists under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY
4.0). Under articles 9, 10 and 13 of the Re-use of Public Sector Information Act (Cap. 546), we
request:

1. **Written confirmation** that this licence allows us to republish the individual entries of
   these registers on practitioner pages, with attribution.
2. **A machine-readable copy** (CSV, Excel or XML) of the following published fields, for
   practitioners currently registered: name, registration number, register (principal,
   specialist, dental), specialty, and practice address only where the register already publishes
   it. We do not ask for any field the register does not publish.
3. If possible, the same file **monthly**, or a stable link to it.

Every page would credit "Source: Medical Council Malta" with the extraction date. We ask that the
data be supplied free of charge or at marginal cost (art. 15).

**Data protection.** The data are professional data the Council publishes under article 10 of the
Health Care Professions Act (Cap. 464). Our processing rests on legitimate interest (Article
6(1)(f) GDPR): helping patients find a registered practitioner. We inform practitioners under
Article 14 (a notice on every page and in our privacy policy), and honour objections under Article
21.

We look forward to your answer within 20 working days (Cap. 546, art. 9(2)). If the request is
refused in whole or in part, please state the reasons and the means of redress (art. 12).

Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Postal address: {{PUBLISHER_ADDRESS}}.

Yours faithfully,

{{PUBLICATION_DIRECTOR}}, for {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}
