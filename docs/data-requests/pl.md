# Poland: what is imported, what is not, and whom to ask

> Prepared 2026-11-27 for issue #164. Not legal advice: every licence below was read on dane.gov.pl
> (the dataset's licence and reuse conditions, through its API), on eTERYT and on the publishers'
> own pages that day.

Poland opened (`/pl`) with healthcare establishments only: the zakłady lecznicze of the healthcare
entities (podmioty lecznicze) of RPWDL, the register of entities performing medical activity
([data import](../data-import.md#poland), [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-multi-country.md#poland-pl)). No
health professional is listed: the individual and group practices of doctors, dentists, nurses,
midwives and physiotherapists (praktyki zawodowe), the register's other part, wait for a Polish
legal opinion on listing named practitioners without their consent (NSA III OSK 1052/21).

Fields never read into a row: the entities' tax and statistical numbers (NIP, REGON), register book
numbers, seats, phones, e-mail addresses and websites, the establishments' and units' e-mail
addresses and websites, a natural person's entity name (a sole trader's business name is their
first and last name) and a natural person's units' names. An establishment's name loses the name of
the natural person who runs it where the register gives it (4,258 names of the 27 November 2026
file), and is replaced by its address when it was that name only (64).
