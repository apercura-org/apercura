## Before sending

- **The data are already open.** Terviseamet publishes, through MEDRE (run by TEHIK), free XML
  files of healthcare providers, their licensed service addresses and the health workers
  registered with each provider (code, name, profession, specialty), and of GP lists with their
  consultation addresses (VERIFIED by download). No phone field. The Health Services Organisation
  Act (TTKS) § 26⁴(9) has the Health Board publish these data "for public use".
- **The only obstacle is the licence.** andmed.eesti.ee lists **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0**. Apercura is free
  and non-profit, but the NC term binds the operator (a future funding model could be read as
  commercial) and the SA term would bind data Apercura adds. The letter asks for CC BY 4.0, or
  failing that a written confirmation that the current licence allows our use. § 3¹ requires
  re-use conditions to be objective, proportionate and non-discriminatory.
- **Public sector body: yes**, a state agency under the Ministry of Social Affairs.

## Letter (English)

**Subject: Re-use of the MEDRE open data: request concerning the licence (Public Information Act,
§ 3¹)**

Terviseamet, Paldiski mnt 81, 10614 Tallinn

Dear Sir or Madam,

{{PUBLISHER_NAME}}, {{PUBLISHER_ADDRESS}}, publishes Apercura, a free, non-profit and open-source
directory of health practitioners. Each page shows factual information only, with its source and
date. There are no ratings, no advertising and no paid ranking. Every practitioner can claim their
page to correct or complete it, or object to its publication; an objection is honoured at once and
the entry is never imported again.

The Health Board publishes as MEDRE open data the healthcare providers, their places of service
and the health workers registered with them, and the GP lists. On andmed.eesti.ee their licence is
given as CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.

Under § 3¹ of the Public Information Act, we ask you to:

1. publish these datasets under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)** licence or an
   equivalent open licence; or, if that is not possible,
2. confirm in writing that the current licence allows us to publish the following fields in a free
   directory, with attribution: health worker code, name, profession and specialty, provider name
   and activity licence number, and place-of-service address.

We do not ask for any other data. Every page would credit "Source: Terviseamet, MEDRE" with the
extraction date.

**Data protection.** The data are professional data the Health Board publishes under § 26⁴(9) of
the Health Services Organisation Act. Our processing rests on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)
GDPR): helping patients find a registered practitioner. We inform practitioners under Article 14 (a
notice on every page and in our privacy policy), and honour objections under Article 21.

We look forward to your answer within the time limit of § 18(1) of the Public Information Act. If
the request is refused, please state the reasons and the means of redress.

Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Yours faithfully,

{{PUBLICATION_DIRECTOR}}, for {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}
