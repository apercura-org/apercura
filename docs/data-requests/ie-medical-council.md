## Before sending

- **Public sector body: yes.** The Medical Council is a statutory body (Medical Practitioners Act
  2007, s. 4). In decision RPSI/22/04 (12 December 2024) the Information Commissioner treated it
  as a public sector body under S.I. 376/2021 and the Council did not contest it (VERIFIED).
- **What the request can and cannot cover.** Re-use rights attach only to documents that are
  already accessible (RPSI/22/04, §18 and §22). The published register holds name, registration
  number, registration type, qualifications, specialty and conditions, and **no practice address
  or phone** (s. 43 and s. 56 of the 2007 Act; s. 56(2) lets the Council withhold home
  addresses). **There is no legal basis to ask for addresses or phones**: the letter asks only for
  the published fields. Practitioners add their practice address when they claim their page.
- **Precedent.** RPSI/22/04 annulled the Council's refusal for the fields its register database
  exports with a built-in tool ("extraction of data from an existing database", not creating a
  document) and ordered a fresh decision. The letter asks for exactly that export.
- The request must say it is made for re-use (reg. 6(1)): the subject line and first paragraph do.

## Letter

**Subject: Request for re-use of public sector information: Register of Medical Practitioners
(S.I. No. 376/2021, regulation 6)**

To the Data Protection Officer and the officer responsible for re-use requests,
Medical Council, Kingram House, Kingram Place, Dublin 2, D02 XY88

Dear Sir or Madam,

This is a request for the **re-use of public sector information** under regulation 6 of the
European Union (Open Data and Re-use of Public Sector Information) Regulations 2021 (S.I. No.
376/2021).

{{PUBLISHER_NAME}}, {{PUBLISHER_ADDRESS}}, publishes Apercura, a free, non-profit and open-source
directory of health practitioners. Each page shows factual information only, with its source and
date. There are no ratings, no advertising and no paid ranking. Every practitioner can claim their
page to correct or complete it, or object to its publication; an objection is honoured at once and
the entry is never imported again.

**Data requested.** From the published Register of Medical Practitioners, for registered
practitioners only: name, registration number, registration type and status, specialty (with
specialist division). We do not ask for any field the register does not publish, and in
particular no address or phone number.

**Format and frequency.** A CSV or Excel export from the register database, as in decision
RPSI/22/04 of the Information Commissioner, once and then monthly if possible (or an equivalent
machine-readable file or interface).

**Licence.** We ask for the data under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0) or
an equivalent open licence, with any conditions kept objective, proportionate and
non-discriminatory (reg. 10). Every page would credit "Source: Medical Council, Register of Medical
Practitioners" with the extraction date. We ask that the data be supplied free of charge or at
marginal cost (reg. 8).

**Data protection.** The data are professional data the Council publishes under sections 43 and 56
of the Medical Practitioners Act 2007. Our processing rests on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)
GDPR): helping patients find a registered doctor. We inform practitioners under Article 14 (a
notice on every page and in our privacy policy), and honour objections under Article 21. We are
willing to sign a re-use agreement setting out these safeguards.

We look forward to your decision within 20 working days (reg. 6(2)). If the request is refused in
whole or in part, please state the reasons and the means of redress (reg. 6(5)-(6)).

Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Yours faithfully,

{{PUBLICATION_DIRECTOR}}, for {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}
