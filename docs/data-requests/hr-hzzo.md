## Before sending

- **Public sector body: yes.** HZZO is a "javna ustanova" with public powers (Zakon o obveznom
  zdravstvenom osiguranju, art. 97; VERIFIED) and a "tijelo javne vlasti" under ZPPI, art. 5(2).
- **What is published.** HZZO publishes XLSX lists of contracted primary care (general practice,
  paediatrics, gynaecology, dental care; October 2026): doctor surname and name, activity, practice
  status, ownership, number of insured persons, **practice address**, codes; no phone (VERIFIED). No
  statute was found that makes these lists a public register; they are published as public
  information.
- **The licence is the issue.** The data.gov.hr copies show "Nije navedena" (`license_id: null`)
  and link to 2018 files, while Pravilnik NN 67/2017, art. 4(4), says data on the open-data portal
  are published under the open licence, and HZZO's own Otvorena dozvola allows commercial re-use
  with attribution (VERIFIED). The letter asks HZZO to confirm the open licence for the current
  files and to fix the portal metadata.
- ZPPI, art. 30(1)(1), excludes information restricted under art. 15 (including personal data);
  these lists are already published by HZZO, so access is not restricted.

## Letter (English)

**Subject: Request for re-use of information: lists of contracted primary-care doctors (Right of
Access to Information Act, arts. 27 and 29)**

Croatian Health Insurance Fund, for the attention of the information officer and the data
protection officer
Margaretska 3, 10000 Zagreb

Dear Sir or Madam,

{{PUBLISHER_NAME}}, {{PUBLISHER_ADDRESS}}, publishes Apercura, a free, non-profit and open-source
directory of health practitioners. Each page shows factual information only, with its source and
date. There are no ratings, no advertising and no paid ranking. Every practitioner can claim their
page to correct or complete it, or object to its publication; an objection is honoured at once and
the entry is never imported again.

**Information requested.** Under articles 27 and 29 of the Right of Access to Information Act, we
request to re-use the lists of contracted primary-care doctors (general and family medicine,
paediatrics, women's health, dental care) that HZZO publishes on its website, with these fields:
doctor's surname and name, activity, name of the health centre or practice, and practice address.
We do not ask for any other data.

**Format and frequency.** The existing XLSX or CSV files, at the update frequency HZZO already
follows.

**Licence.** We ask you to confirm that the open licence applies to these files (Ordinance on open
licences, NN 67/2017, art. 4) or the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0), and to
state that licence for the datasets on data.gov.hr, which currently read "Nije navedena" (not
specified). Every page would credit "Source: HZZO" with the download date. We ask that the
information be provided free of charge (art. 32).

**Data protection.** The data are professional data HZZO publishes. Our processing rests on
legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): helping patients find a contracted doctor. We inform
practitioners under Article 14 (a notice on every page and in our privacy policy), and honour
objections under Article 21.

We look forward to your answer within 15 days (art. 29(4)). If the request is refused, please
state the reasons and the means of redress.

Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Yours faithfully,

{{PUBLICATION_DIRECTOR}}, for {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}
