> **DRAFT — not legal advice. To be reviewed and completed by a lawyer and the DPO before booking opens to patients.**

Two records are kept: the record of the publisher as **controller** (art. 30(1)) and its
record as **processor** for online booking (art. 30(2)). The analysis behind them is in the
[DPIA](dpia-draft.md). File paths refer to the repository state of 22 November 2026; facts
marked **to confirm** could not be verified in the code or the documentation.
