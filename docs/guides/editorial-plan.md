# Guides: editorial plan

The guides (`/fr/guides`, `/fr/guides/{slug}`) are short French articles that answer the
practical questions people have when they look for a practitioner: what a consultation will
cost, how to be reimbursed, who to call at night. They also answer practitioners' questions
about their page. The format and validation are described in
[public-site.md](../public-site.md#guides).

This plan was researched on **2026-11-25**. The source facts for the first batch, page by
page, are in the facts dossier handed to the writers. Its key points are summarised here.

**What this leaves open for us.** The official pages are accurate but split into
procedures. The commercial pages are current on some topics but sell a service. Nobody
puts these three things together: the rule in plain French, the official page that states
it, and the reader's next step (call, check the sector, declare a médecin traitant,
correct one's data). Several current facts are also often missed:

- 116 117 is not nationwide. The ARS picks 116 117 or 15 for each region (R6315-3 CSP).
- The midwives' code of deontology was renumbered on 1 January 2026.
- The new Ameli annuaire no longer shows prices.

## Topics

Search queries below are **inferred** from official FAQs, "People also ask"-style
questions and the way the official pages phrase things. They are not measured. No search
volumes are given because none were measured.

Internal links use the listing pattern `/fr/{listing-slug}` (for example `/fr/medecin-generaliste`),
`/fr/annuaire` and practitioner pages `/fr/praticien/{slug}`. The `listings` frontmatter of a
guide decides which listing and practitioner pages show it under "Guides utiles".

### 1. Médecin traitant: choosing, declaring, changing (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « comment déclarer un médecin traitant », « changer de médecin traitant », « pas de médecin traitant remboursement », « médecin traitant enfant moins de 16 ans », « aucun médecin n'accepte de nouveaux patients ».
- **Competitors:** ameli and service-public cover it fully, on two or three pages. The sante.fr article has outdated figures.
- **Our angle:** one page, from "why" to "how", with ameli's 30 € example (19 € reimbursed instead of 8,40 €). It lists the direct-access exceptions and says what to do when no doctor accepts you, as service-public says it: the organisation coordonnée territoriale, then the caisse. We do not present the médiateur as the route for this case.
- **Links:** `/fr/medecin-generaliste`, `/fr/pediatre`, `/fr/gynecologue`, `/fr/annuaire`.

### 2. Fees: conventionnement, secteur 1 and 2, OPTAM, dépassements (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « secteur 1 secteur 2 différence », « médecin OPTAM c'est quoi », « dépassement d'honoraires remboursé mutuelle », « comment savoir si un médecin est conventionné », « médecin non conventionné remboursement ».
- **Competitors:** ameli, service-public (F17042, whose check date is 22 Dec 2024), Qare, Doctolib's help centre, sante.fr (2019).
- **Our angle:** what each status means for the bill. It covers the information the patient must get: fees shown in the waiting room, written information once the dépassement reaches 70 € (arrêté du 30 mai 2018), fee information online (R4127-53). It explains the Complémentaire santé solidaire rule, the contrat responsable cap (R871-2 CSS), and how to check a sector in the Ameli annuaire (sector and option, no longer prices).
- **Links:** `/fr/medecin-generaliste`, `/fr/gynecologue`, `/fr/pediatre`, practitioner pages (they show the sector from Ameli data).

### 3. Carte Vitale and the appli carte Vitale (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « activer appli carte Vitale », « appli carte Vitale sans France Identité », « carte Vitale perdue », « feuille de soins papier délai », « le médecin n'accepte pas l'appli carte Vitale ».
- **Competitors:** ameli (complete, several pages). France Identité's news page is out of date.
- **Our angle:** who can activate the app and how: France Identité or video, 16 and over, and, since 28 May 2026, 16–17 year olds attached to a parent. It also covers what the app does not do (no medical information, not accepted everywhere yet, so keep the card), a lost card, paper forms (2 years), and the basics of tiers payant. Practitioner pages show carte Vitale acceptance (with the sector and OPTAM) when the page is matched with the Ameli directory; they do not say whether the app is accepted, so the guide must not imply it.
- **Links:** `/fr/medecin-generaliste`, `/fr/annuaire`.

### 4. Dental care and 100 % Santé (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « 100 % santé dentaire c'est quoi », « couronne remboursée à 100 % », « devis dentiste obligatoire », « M'T dents âge », « dentiste conventionné ».
- **Competitors:** ameli (very complete), service-public F33956, Doctolib's oral health section, Doctissimo dossier.
- **Our angle:** soins conservateurs and prostheses, explained separately. It covers the three baskets, what the devis must contain, that 100 % Santé is offered by every dentist with a contrat responsable, and M'T dents from age 3 to 24. Ceiling prices appear only with their "au 1er janvier 2026" date, or as a link to the ameli table.
- **Links:** `/fr/chirurgien-dentiste`.

### 5. Home nursing care (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « infirmière à domicile remboursement », « faut-il une ordonnance pour une infirmière », « infirmier à domicile prix déplacement », « trouver une infirmière libérale », « vaccin infirmier sans ordonnance ».
- **Competitors:** Doctolib Santé has dedicated articles. ameli has no patient page; the rules are in its nurses' section.
- **Our angle:** filling that gap: the prescription and what it must say (home, night, Sunday), 60 % reimbursement (100 % for an ALD or maternity), travel costs, the BSI, vaccination without a prescription from age 11. Direct access under the law of 27 June 2025 appears only once its implementing texts are confirmed on Légifrance. Home visits on Apercura are described as implemented (a service area of communes, a fixed travel time).
- **Links:** `/fr/infirmier`.

### 6. Pregnancy follow-up with a midwife (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « suivi de grossesse sage-femme ou gynécologue », « déclaration de grossesse avant quand », « 7 examens prénataux », « prise en charge 100 % grossesse 6e mois », « entretien prénatal précoce ».
- **Competitors:** ameli, service-public F963, the Ordre des sages-femmes, Doctissimo (large pregnancy section).
- **Our angle:** what a midwife does, from the Ordre's page; the calendar (7 exams, declaration before the end of the 3rd month, 100 % from the 6th month); the "sage-femme référente". On when a doctor takes over, we quote L4151-3 CSP and the HAS recommendation (low risk: midwife or doctor as the woman chooses; high risk: obstetrician-gynaecologist). We give no medical advice.
- **Links:** `/fr/sage-femme`, `/fr/gynecologue`.

### 7. Emergencies and on-duty doctors (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « médecin de garde ce soir », « 116 117 ou 15 », « numéro urgence sourd SMS », « pharmacie de garde », « forfait urgences prix ».
- **Competitors:** service-public F33954 (current), sante.gouv.fr (2023), sante.fr (2019, outdated), Maiia (presents 116 117 as national), Doctissimo dossier.
- **Our angle:** the numbers first, in a list, with no preamble: 15, 112, 114 (SMS, app, video), and 116 117 or 15 depending on the region. Then the SAS through the 15, the permanence des soins hours (R6315-1), on-duty pharmacies through the mairie as service-public says (no 3237: we found no official source), and the FPU of 23 € with its reduced rate and exemptions. When to go to the emergency department is said only in F33954's words.
- **Links:** `/fr/medecin-generaliste`, `/fr/annuaire`.

### 8. How to choose a practitioner (priority: medium, batch 2)

- **Queries (inferred):** « comment choisir son médecin », « trouver un médecin qui prend de nouveaux patients », « vérifier qu'un médecin est inscrit à l'Ordre ».
- **Competitors:** sante.fr (2019), commercial booking sites (built around their own listings).
- **Our angle:** criteria that can be checked: registration in the RPPS, sector and option, carte Vitale acceptance, home visits, accessibility. No ratings, because Apercura has none and deontology forbids testimonials in practitioners' own communication. This guide depends on the first seven and is not in batch 1.
- **Links:** every listing, `/fr/annuaire`.

### 9. How Apercura works (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « Apercura c'est quoi », « d'où viennent les données », « pourquoi ce médecin est sur Apercura ».
- **Competitors:** none; this is our own page.
- **Our angle:** the data sources and their licence (Annuaire Santé/RPPS and FINESS by the ANS, the Ameli annuaire by the CNAM, all under Licence Ouverte 2.0 on data.gouv.fr), refresh cadence, and why each page shows its source and date (RPPS reuse conditions, art. 7 of the arrêté du 23 septembre 2022). It also says why there are no ratings or ads. Apercura's own facts come from the repo and describe only what is implemented.
- **Links:** `/fr/annuaire`, a sample listing.

### 10. For practitioners: claim your page (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « revendiquer sa fiche praticien », « Pro Santé Connect e-CPS activer », « numéro RPPS où le trouver », « ce que j'ai le droit de publier sur internet médecin ».
- **Competitors:** booking platforms' sales pages (not compared here). The ANS documents PSC and e-CPS.
- **Our angle:** what claiming does and how identity is verified (Pro Santé Connect / e-CPS, or a document reviewed by a person, as implemented). It covers what the deontology allows each profession to publish: R4127-19-1 for doctors, R4127-215-1 for dentists, R4127-349 for midwives (since 1 January 2026), R4312-68-1 for nurses. That rules out testimonials and comparisons. It notes the fee information a doctor's page must include (R4127-53).
- **Links:** practitioner pages.

### 11. For practitioners: removal and GDPR rights (priority: high, batch 1)

- **Queries (inferred):** « supprimer ma fiche annuaire médecin », « droit d'opposition RPPS », « corriger mes données RPPS », « plainte CNIL annuaire ».
- **Competitors:** none for patients. The ANS's terms of use and the CNIL's rights pages are the references.
- **Our angle:** two levels, kept apart. At the source, the RPPS: the right to object does not apply (art. 10 of the arrêté du 23 septembre 2022), and corrections go to the registration authority (ordre or ARS), not to the ANS. On Apercura: the rights the CNIL describes (access, rectification, objection, erasure, complaint), and Apercura's removal on request as implemented. No legal conclusion goes beyond what the texts say.
- **Links:** practitioner pages.

## Update cadence

- Every article is reviewed against all its sources **at least every 6 months**. The review sets `reviewedAt`. Changing the text also sets `updatedAt`.
- An article is reviewed early **when one of its facts can change**. Typical triggers:
  - a new convention (médicale, dentaire, infirmière, sages-femmes) or an avenant to one, which changes tariffs, OPTAM and BSI;
  - the yearly social security finance law (LFSS) and its decrees, which change the participation forfaitaire, franchises, the FPU and contrats responsables;
  - tariff changes on 1 January and on the dates set by avenants (for example the nurses' tariffs of 6 November 2026);
  - a new decree or arrêté on a code article we cite (for example the midwives' code renumbered on 1 January 2026, or the implementing texts of the nurses' law of 27 June 2025);
  - a change to an official page we cite: new URL, new "vérifié le" date, a figure that changed;
  - an ARS decision that moves a region between 116 117 and 15.
- Known items to recheck before the first publication: the annual cap of the participation forfaitaire (ameli shows 70 € on one page and 50 € on another), the dentist consultation tariff (23 € shown without a date), and the Annuaire Santé host (`annuaire.sante.fr` did not resolve; `annuaire.esante.gouv.fr` answered).
- A source page that disappears or redirects to a home page is replaced before the next deploy, or the fact it supported is removed.

## Editorial rules

Health content is YMYL: a mistake can cost a reader money or delay care.

**Sources**

- Every factual claim is sourced to an official page: ameli.fr, service-public.gouv.fr, has-sante.fr, legifrance.gouv.fr, the professional orders (conseil-national.medecin.fr, ordre-chirurgiens-dentistes.fr, ordre-sages-femmes.fr, ordre-infirmiers.fr), sante.gouv.fr / solidarites-sante.gouv.fr, esante.gouv.fr and data.gouv.fr for the registries, cnil.fr for GDPR. The allowed hosts are enforced by `OFFICIAL_SOURCE_HOSTS` in `apps/web/src/lib/guides/guide.ts`.
- No secondary sources for facts: no health magazines, no mutuelle or comparison sites, no booking platforms.
- Each source in the frontmatter has its own title as written on the page, its publisher and the date it was checked (`checkedAt`). The article has a `reviewedAt` date.
- Cite the page that states the fact, not a hub page. Prefer the current URL after redirects (service-public.gouv.fr, not service-public.fr; the CNIL's `/fr/comprendre-mes-droits/` pages).
- A legal article is cited by number and code, with its Légifrance link, as in force on the check date.

**Amounts and dates**

- An amount appears only if it is on the official page on the check date, written with the date the page gives ("au 1er janvier 2026"). If the page shows no date or two official pages disagree, leave the amount out and link to the page.
  **What we never do**

- No medical advice: no symptoms to watch for, no "you should see a doctor if…". We say who does what and how to reach them. Clinical questions go to a professional.
- Every article that mentions an emergency points to **15** (Samu), **112** (European number) and **114** (by SMS, app or video, for deaf, deafblind, hard-of-hearing and aphasic people, as service-public says). For the on-duty doctor we write **116 117 or 15, depending on the region**, never 116 117 alone.
- No promotional claims about Apercura: no "the best", "the only", "revolutionary". We state facts about Apercura only as they are implemented in the code. We never claim a certification or a usage figure that does not exist (see [PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md)).
- No comparison with or criticism of other services by name in the articles.
- No ratings, rankings or "top 10" lists of practitioners.
- No AI-sounding filler: no « Dans un monde où… », « Il est important de noter que… », « En conclusion… », and no empty closing paragraph. An article ends with what the reader can do next.

**Plain French (FALC-friendly)**

- Short sentences, one idea per sentence. Active voice. "Vous".
- Every technical term is defined at first use, in the same sentence or the next: « le ticket modérateur, c'est la part qui reste à votre charge », « OPTAM (option de pratique tarifaire maîtrisée) ».
- The answer comes first, then the details. Headings are questions or tasks (« Comment déclarer votre médecin traitant ? »).
- Say what the reader can do next, with a link: declare, call, check the sector, ask for a devis, contact the caisse.

**Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA, RGAA 4.1)**

- Heading order: the title is the only h1. Sections use `##`, subsections `###`, with no skipped levels (the validator accepts only `##` and `###`).
- Link text says where it goes: « la page de l'Assurance Maladie sur le 100 % Santé », never « cliquez ici » or a bare URL.
- No information is given only in a table or only by colour. A table is always preceded by a sentence that gives its key point. Keep tables small (three columns at most) so they read on a phone.
- Lists for steps and numbers; phone numbers written in full (« 15 », « 114 »).
- Restricted Markdown only (headings, paragraphs, lists, blockquotes, tables, emphasis, links to `https:`, `/fr/…` or `#anchors`), as enforced by the validator. No raw HTML, no images in batch 1.

**Review**

- A second person checks every amount, date and quote against the source page before merge, and sets `checkedAt` to the day they checked it.
- A fact that cannot be confirmed on an official page is removed, not softened.
